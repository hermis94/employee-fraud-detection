02 / METHOD

Unlabelled.
Not unexamined.

A one-class anomaly workflow ranks claims by how unlike the observed expense mix they appear.

01

Build the claim table

4,500 deterministic synthetic claims include category, department, vendor, MWK amount, receipt indicators, submission timing, recent volume, and tenure.

02

Prepare mixed features

One-hot encode the categorical dimensions and robust-scale numeric and binary values. Employee and claim IDs are excluded.

03

Isolate unusual patterns

Fit a 400-tree Isolation Forest. Its 4.5% contamination setting is a demonstration review-capacity assumption, not an estimated fraud rate.

04

Rank for human review

Sort claims by anomaly score; higher values are more unusual. Export a flagged queue with context fields for an investigator to assess.
